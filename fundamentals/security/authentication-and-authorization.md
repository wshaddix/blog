# Authentication and Authorization

Authentication answers who the caller is. Authorization answers what that caller may do. The two checks fail in different ways.

*This page is an outline. The sections will be filled in over time, including a C# sample where the topic has one.*

## Identity

A user, a service, and a device are different callers.

## Credentials

Passwords, keys, and tokens. What each one proves.

## Sessions and tokens

A cookie that the browser stores, and a token that the caller presents.

## Authorization

Roles, claims, and a check against a specific resource.

## Where the check lives

The UI can hide a button. The server still has to refuse the call.
