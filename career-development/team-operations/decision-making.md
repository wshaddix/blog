# Decision Making

A technical choice can be proposed by someone who will not be the one to enforce it.

## Intent proposals

- TODO: The proposal names the problem, the options, and the tradeoff. It is not a finished design dropped on the team.
- TODO: Reviewers challenge the intent. The author writes the decision after that, in a form the team can find later.

## Decision records

- TODO: Keep a short record of what was chosen, what was rejected, and which new fact would reopen it.
- TODO: Put the record where the code it governs lives, so a later change can find the reason.

## Automated guardrails

- TODO: Turn the decision into a check that fails the build when new code breaks it.
- TODO: A record with no check is a note. The check is what keeps the decision in force after the meeting.
