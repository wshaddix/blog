# Debugging

How to find a defect: reproduce it, narrow it, fix it, and prove the fix.

*This page is an outline. The sections will be filled in over time, including a C# sample where the topic has one.*

## Make it happen again

A failing test, a request, or a set of steps.

## Narrow the cause

What changed, what the inputs were, and which layer is wrong.

## The debugger

Breakpoints, watches, and stepping through an async call.

## Logs and traces

Correlation ids when the debugger cannot reach the process.

## Lock the fix in

A test that failed before the change and passes after it.
